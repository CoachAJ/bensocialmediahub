> Source: https://buffer.com/pricing
> Buffer is a suite of tools for social media management — publishing, analytics, engagement, and AI assistant.

We've built simpler social media tools for busy people. Enjoy our free plan as you get started, or trial our full toolkit priced to make marketing your business on social affordable.

## Plans

Up to 3 channels.

- **1 user account**: The number of users who can access and create content in your Buffer account.
- **AI Assistant**: Unlimited use of our AI to create, refine, and repurpose content (optional).
- **Insights**: Follower growth, engagement, impressions, and post performance — with 30 days of history.

- **World-class customer support**: Fast and helpful support via email.

## Volume Pricing

## FAQs

### What counts as a channel?

A channel is one social account you connect to Buffer — a single Instagram profile, Facebook page, LinkedIn page, TikTok account, and so on. You're billed per channel, so you only pay for the accounts you actually manage. If you run five brands with an Instagram and a Facebook page each, that's 10 channels.

### What are the billing options for Buffer's paid plans?

You can choose to pay for your plan on a monthly or annual basis. By selecting annual billing, you'll receive a 20% discount. You can switch between monthly and annual billing at any time. If you switch billing frequencies, any necessary prorations or refunds will be automatically applied.

### What payment methods does Buffer accept?

We accept credit and debit card payments from Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, Discover, and Diners Club. Unfortunately, we cannot accept PayPal, checks, or bank transfers at this time. [Learn more about our accepted payment methods](https://support.buffer.com/articles/buffers-accepted-payment-methods-PQpO40rDXV?utm_source=marketing-site&utm_medium=help-center&utm_campaign=help-center).

### Does Buffer have a free trial?

Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial for our paid plans. During the trial, you can upgrade to a paid plan or switch to our Free plan at any time. You can also start using Buffer for free right away by signing up for our Free plan without needing a trial period.

### What happens at the end of the 14-day trial?

If you decide to continue with a subscription after your trial, simply enter your payment details under the "Billing Method" section in your account settings. If you don't wish to upgrade and haven't entered your payment details, your account will automatically be downgraded to the Free plan.

Any scheduled posts will still be published after the trial ends, provided the connected social channels are not locked. Channels will be locked if you exceed the three-channel limit for the Free plan.

### Can I keep my three free channels if I upgrade from Buffer's Free plan to a paid plan?

If you decide to upgrade to a paid plan, you will pay for each channel — including any channels you had while on the Free plan. This is because paid plans offer unlimited usage and advanced features for all channels you've connected to Buffer.

### What is the scheduled post limit for each plan?

The Free plan allows 10 scheduled posts per channel. Once a post is published, the slot becomes available again, up to the 10-post maximum per channel.

For paid plans, you have unlimited scheduled posts per channel. Unlimited scheduling is subject to our fair use policy, which caps usage to 5000 posts per channel. We describe this as "unlimited" because no one has ever exceeded this cap.

### Does Buffer offer a discount to nonprofit organizations and charities?

Yes, Buffer proudly offers a 50% discount on paid plans for nonprofit and charity organizations. [Learn more about our nonprofit discount](https://support.buffer.com/articles/buffers-nonprofit-discount-5DWYhyusYn?utm_source=marketing-site&utm_medium=help-center&utm_campaign=help-center).

### Does Buffer charge Value-Added Tax (VAT) to customers?

Buffer applies the local standard Value-Added Tax (VAT) rate for B2C customer located in the United Kingdom. B2B customers may be eligible for VAT exclusion through reverse charge. You can enter your VAT number to have VAT excluded from your invoice. [Learn more about taxes and VAT](https://support.buffer.com/articles/why-was-i-charged-that-your-bill-taxes-and-vat-explained-YMilE1mo8m).

Our pricing was last updated in November 2025. [Learn more about what goes into Buffer's pricing](https://support.buffer.com/articles/what-are-the-benefits-of-buffers-new-plans-JfbH35SXXn).

### How does Buffer's new pricing help me save as I grow?

Buffer uses volume discounts for channels. That means your price per channel gets lower once you manage more channels, so adding more social profiles scales more affordably.

In practice, channels 1–10 are priced at the standard rate, and any channels above 10 cost less per channel. Your total may increase as you add channels, but your average cost per channel goes down as you grow.

This was designed for creators, teams, and agencies who manage lots of social accounts and want predictable, scalable pricing, without the cost jumping linearly forever.
