Pharmacist Ben's Health Challenge
What is a major limitation of epidemiological dietary studies compared to empirical laboratory research?
Biological Insight:

Epidemiological studies draw statistical associations based on self-reported questionnaires and cannot account for all confounding variables like lifestyle, exercise, or environmental stressors, unlike empirical laboratory studies that prove direct biological mechanisms.
